This calculator is useful because it keeps the focus on **apples-to-apples digital comparisons** rather than dramatic but mismatched analogies. It was also an early blueprint for the comparison frame on this site. The two tools diverge in method and activity mix, but they share the instinct to put AI beside the rest of digital life and then send users back to sources.
