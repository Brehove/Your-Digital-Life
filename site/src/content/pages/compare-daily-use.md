The scenario records put the non-AI student at 132 Wh/day of server-side energy, the medium AI student at 119 Wh/day, the vibe coder at 402 Wh/day, and the hosted-Zoom faculty profile at 223 Wh/day. Their main job is to make relative scale visible, not to imply that one profile is morally clean and another is not.
