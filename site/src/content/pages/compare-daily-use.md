The scenario records put the non-AI student at 132 Wh/day of server and network energy, the medium AI student at 119 Wh/day, the vibe coder at 2,626.675 Wh/day, and the hosted-Zoom faculty profile at 223 Wh/day. The vibe-coder starter includes 15 illustrative agentic tasks, counted as 15 human coding-agent requests at 150 Wh each, for 2,250 Wh in coding requests. These are illustrative requests, not verified completed tasks. Its eight high-reasoning prompts total 270.4 Wh. These are modeled comparisons, not measured personal footprints.
